## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an executive-grade infrastructure command aesthetic tailored for mission-critical power distribution, grid telemetry, and utility billing operations. It conveys operational authority, fiscal transparency, and sovereign-grade reliability.

The design language balances high-density utility ergonomics with an architectural, institutional presence. It avoids frivolous decorative gradients in favor of sharp structural definition, razor-thin technical borders, micro-status indicators, and precise spatial pacing. Data density is treated as a first-class citizen: information is prioritized through deliberate tonal contrast, systematic typographic weight shifts, and disciplined grid partitioning. The overall aesthetic feels dependable, technically rigorous, and uncompromisingly modern.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for high-legibility telemetry, billing certainty, and instant state discrimination across complex enterprise layouts.

### Primary & Brand Surfaces
- **Deep Electric Navy (`#0F2744`)**: The anchor of the design system. Used for core structural chrome, navigation rails, high-priority command buttons, and institutional headings.
- **Deep Navy Tint / Hover (`#1E3A8A`)**: Secondary focus states, active navigation indicators, and primary surface elevation shifts.
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`)**: A cool, clinical light backdrop that minimizes eye strain during extended operational shifts.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white cards and data containers, establishing high-contrast reading planes.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#F1F5F9`)**: Sub-tables, metric headers, and inset monitoring wells.

### Functional Accents & Telemetry
- **Electric Cyan (`#0284C7`)**: Active flow, live telemetry meters, line-loss indicators, and primary interactive links.
- **Power Cerulean (`#2563EB`)**: Interactive actions, batch export triggers, and active tab highlights.

### Status & State Telemetry
- **Operational / Paid (`#059669` / Surface `#ECFDF5`)**: Terminal status for settled invoices, balanced feeders, and active sub-stations.
- **Pending / In-Transit (`#D97706` / Surface `#FFFBEB`)**: Unreconciled meter cycles, provisional payments, and warning thresholds.
- **Critical Alert / Overdue (`#DC2626` / Surface `#FEF2F2`)**: Breached feeder limits, disconnected service lines, and overdue industrial receivables.

### Neutral Scale
- **Text Primary (`#0F172A`)**: High-contrast body, tabular monetary readouts, and labels.
- **Text Secondary (`#475569`)**: Supporting metadata, unit indicators, and field descriptions.
- **Border / Divider (`#CBD5E1`)**: Subtle structural division lines and container perimeters.
- **Border Subtle (`#E2E8F0`)**: Table row borders and nested module segmentations.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs `Manrope` for structured titles and section heads with `Hanken Grotesk` for data density, form controls, and tabular reports.

All currency readouts featuring the Indian Rupee symbol (`₹`), megawatt-hour figures (`MWh`), and kilowatt-hour metrics (`kWh`) must enforce tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`). This maintains precise columnar alignment across balance sheets, grid generation tables, and tariff breakdowns.

Labels and badge captions utilize slight letter-spacing expansions (`0.04em`) in all-caps or title-case to preserve immediate legibility at small sizes (11px). Headings should prioritize concise phrasing and avoid multiline wrapping within operational dashboards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column responsive fluid grid system designed for uninterrupted enterprise monitoring across diverse display aspect ratios.

- **Desktop (1280px and above)**: 12-column layout with `margin-desktop` (32px) and `gutter-desktop` (24px). Accommodates persistent left-rail navigation (260px fixed) alongside fluid multi-panel data grids.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout with `gutter` (16px) and `margin` (24px). Left-rail navigation collapses to an icon-only dock (72px) with horizontal sub-headers.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column layout with `gutter` (16px) and `margin` (16px). Left rail collapses into a slide-over off-canvas drawer. Key monitoring cards transition from multi-column tiles to stacked single-column feeds.

Components adhere strictly to an 8px grid interval, using 4px micro-increments (`space-xs`) strictly for chip padding, indicator tags, and input icon offsets.

## Elevation & Depth

The design system implements a disciplined architectural elevation model that relies on crisp perimeter definition rather than heavy ambient diffusion.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: Ground floor (`#F8FAFC`). Flat, unbordered background.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Shells)**: Surface `#FFFFFF` encased in a crisp 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) with an anchor shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 39, 68, 0.05), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 39, 68, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Flyouts & Filter Popovers)**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `#CBD5E1`, with elevation shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 39, 68, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 39, 68, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Critical Incident Overlays)**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `#CBD5E1`, supported by a dark scrim (`#0F2744` at 45% opacity) and shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 39, 68, 0.16), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 39, 68, 0.10)`.

Inset wells (used for raw log streams or sensor meter readouts) use `#F1F5F9` with a subtle inner edge line (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 39, 68, 0.06)`) to convey physical housing.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a calibrated "Soft" geometric profile (`roundedness: 1`). 

- Standard inputs, buttons, and telemetry tiles feature `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii.
- Large operational surfaces, cards, and modal panels use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Navigation flyouts and drawer edges scale up to `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px).
- Pill shapes are reserved exclusively for contextual status pills, phase flags, and feeder connection tags.

This controlled, restrained curvature conveys enterprise precision and mechanical reliability, aligning with physical utility control panels.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action**: Deep Electric Navy (`#0F2744`) background with crisp white typography. Hover states elevate to `#1E3A8A`. Height: 38px (desktop), 44px (touch/mobile).
- **Secondary / Actionable**: Clean white fill, 1px border (`#CBD5E1`), text `#0F172A`. On hover, surface shifts to `#F8FAFC` with border `#94A3B8`.
- **Telemetry Action (Utility)**: Electric Cyan (`#0284C7`) background with white typography for live sync, grid switching, or dynamic load balancing triggers.

### Cards & Container Panels
- Surface white with strict 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border. Headers feature a distinct bottom border separation, housing title, subtitle, and an inline status or action cluster. Padding defaults to `space-md` (16px) for high density, expanding to `space-lg` (24px) on executive overview dashboards.

### Status Badges & Telemetry Chips
- Compact, high-readability tags with light-tinted backgrounds and solid typography:
  - **Paid / Active**: Text `#059669`, background `#ECFDF5`, border `#A7F3D0`.
  - **Pending / Due**: Text `#D97706`, background `#FFFBEB`, border `#FDE68A`.
  - **Overdue / Critical**: Text `#DC2626`, background `#FEF2F2`, border `#FECACA`.
  - **Grid Phase / Neutral**: Text `#0F2744`, background `#F1F5F9`, border `#CBD5E1`.

### Data Tables & Lists
- Table headers use `#F8FAFC` background with uppercase `label-sm` typography (`#475569`). Row height is locked to 48px for compact density, separating rows with 1px `#F1F5F9`. Hover state shifts row surface to `#F8FAFC`. All numeric columns (kWh, load factor, billing in ₹) align right with `tabular-nums`.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Single-line inputs feature 1px solid border (`#CBD5E1`), 38px height, `#FFFFFF` surface, and text `#0F172A`. Active focus produces a 1px border in `#0284C7` paired with a 2px outer glow (`rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)`). Input labels consistently use `label-md` directly above the field with `space-xs` gap.

### Selection Controls
- Checkboxes and radio targets use a 16px square/circle footprint with a 1.5px border (`#94A3B8`). Selected states fill with `#0F2744` and display crisp white check/dot indicators.
